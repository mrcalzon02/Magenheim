# First Bloom recessed radial shelves, revision 8

The user's circular shape guide corrects the compressed upper fins in revision 7. This pass moves the short fleshy join to the cavity center and distributes 22 lamellae around it. Upper fins spread across the roof rather than collecting at the sides. Middle and lower fins radiate outward with gentle downward folds.

The frills are deep shelves: narrow visible leading edges, broad faces extending backward toward the recessed cavity bowl, and supported joins at the center and wall. They retain the pale fleshy material and recessed amber backing. Front, angled and more oblique clay comparisons show the full fan and shelf depth separately. The original concept crop and the user's shape guide are included as references.

This pass reshapes existing vertices without adding triangles: 9,252 triangles, 1.491× the 6,206-triangle Troll donor, within the 9,309 soft cap. It keeps one mesh, one material and four 512px atlas maps. Previous revisions are preserved.

All 120 marked structural attachment ends intersect their designated support in the saved rest mesh. Five inherited attack samples passed the 1mm contact tolerance, with maximum outside separation of 0.52mm. Offline source geometry, atlas, weights, retained actions and exported triangle checks passed. These checks do not establish visual acceptance, all-animation deformation quality or native runtime performance. This remains an uninstalled art study, with intersecting attachment surfaces rather than welded topology.
