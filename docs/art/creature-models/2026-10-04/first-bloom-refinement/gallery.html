Caps have a shared rolled rim and folded underside instead of hundreds of detached gill tubes. Bark ribs are part of the trunk and limb surfaces. Elbows and knees share continuous weighted surfaces; palm masses seat the root fingers. Chest gills are two closed folded panels. Owned materials and fine gill normals are baked into one atlas.

This is a cost and coherence pilot, not final art or live performance acceptance. The texture atlas still needs authored local detail and seam review. The GLB is one skinned primitive; the current production creature path uses rigid parts and cannot load this study directly. No runtime admission or installation was made.

Views use the preceding gallery’s lighting, exposure, camera directions and framing rule. Native bind-pose materials are approximate. Detail views have individual focus points. Five sampled attack frames passed finite-coordinate/bounds checks; that does not prove good animation deformation.
