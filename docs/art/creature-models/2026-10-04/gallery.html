Six bosses — first construction pass

Authored shape and material studies. Not installed or accepted as finished game creatures.

Compare each boss with its committed concept. The sources keep the existing species rig and action names as a retargeting baseline. The GLBs show the rest pose; inherited pose images are offline inspection aids.

The First Bloom

Blender sourceRest-pose GLB

Asymmetric shelf crown, exposed ribbed gills, living arm cords, root feet and separate talons. Next: reduce cylindrical joins and add finer underside folds.

The Blackwater Maw

Blender sourceRest-pose GLB

Thick open jaw vault, modeled trap teeth, inner throat, complete fin webs, sensory organs and a tapered body. Next: wedge-shaped skull and smoother jaw-to-throat joins.

The Furnace Heart

Blender sourceRest-pose GLB

Wide broken masonry boiler cage, exposed molten chamber, heavy impact fists and unequal chimney stacks. Next: fracture larger slabs and vary erosion.

The White Silence

Blender sourceRest-pose GLB

Hollow narrow spine, hanging shard mantle, long ice claws and a cleft vertical crown. Next: replace the provisional face veil and inspect shoulder deformation.

The Rift Titan

Blender sourceRest-pose GLB

Suspended segmented slabs, sparse binding cage and split crown menhirs. Next: introduce larger irregular masses, more pronounced voids and asymmetric shoulder strata.

The Carrion Crown

Blender sourceRest-pose GLB

One massive rooted trunk, radial bone crown, exposed amber organ, flared roots and hanging seed pods. Next: make the crown less radial and articulate the branching tendrils.

Production continues through boss anatomy and deformation refinement, then large apex/elite creatures, then medium and small creatures. Collider, hitbox, LOD, native-material, locomotion, attack and multiplayer acceptance remain pending.
