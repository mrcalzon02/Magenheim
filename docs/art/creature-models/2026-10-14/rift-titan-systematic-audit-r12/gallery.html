Head front

H01 · P0 · Head silhouette is a leaf

Concept: A crooked angular crystal with an uneven peak, stepped sides and a broken taper.

Current: r12 is a smooth, almost bilateral lens with a clean point and continuous curved-looking sides.

Fix: Rebuild the outer profile from the front reference landmarks while retaining one connected crystal and an inset fissure. Confidence: high; open.

H02 · P0 · Head lacks fractured surface construction

Concept: Long irregular fracture planes, overlapping angular ledges and local chips break up the head surface.

Current: A handful of broad uninterrupted planes wrap seven regular cross-sections.

Fix: Sculpt large secondary fracture planes and steps into the same shell before adding small texture cracks. Confidence: high; open.

H03 · P0 · Fissure reads as an inlaid stripe

Concept: The violet reveal changes width and direction, with broken uneven lips and locally opened fracture faces.

Current: The narrow near-vertical purple ribbon and its bevels run with almost uniform visual rhythm.

Fix: Shape an irregular fault path, variable opening and asymmetric bevel depth; keep purple behind the lips and a solid rear wall. Confidence: high; open.

H04 · P1 · Lower head reveal is missing

Concept: The lower head has a pronounced broken angular taper and wider exposed violet fracture detail.

Current: The leaf closes into a neat dark taper with a small terminating purple strip.

Fix: Match the lower fracture silhouette and violet reveal without making two separate head pieces. Confidence: high; open.

H05 · P1 · Head profile is too uniformly slab-like

Concept: The side and three-quarter drawings show irregular crystal thickness and changing fracture planes.

Current: The current profile has one long blade face and a simple continuous back.

Fix: Vary depth and the side planes at the actual reference break points; calibrate the oblique comparison before choosing a thickness change. Confidence: medium; open.

H06 · P2 · Head surface has no useful crystal/stone hierarchy

Concept: Sharp dark seams, brighter facets, chipped gray/brown faces and violet recess contrast distinguish the head.

Current: The same soft cloudy surface covers most of the head, obscuring any small facet variation.

Fix: Author the head surface around its fracture planes, with deliberate edge, recess and violet contrast. Confidence: high; open.

M07 · P2 · Important head detail has very little atlas area

Concept: The head fissure needs a legible irregular material transition.

Current: Its purple floor currently totals only about 32 nominal 512px atlas texels of UV area; this is not 32 pixels per face.

Fix: Reallocate texel density and simplify low-value hidden UVs within the current map budget after the head shape is correct. Confidence: high; open.

Process failures

Q01 · Comparison framing is not calibrated

Different perspective and pose were treated too much like matched orthographic drawings.

Fix: Use the frontal reference for primary landmarks, the same left-facing side for depth, and label angle-dependent judgments as provisional.

Q02 · No standing-plane review caught the grounding defect

A free-floating preview hid the shins below foot level and gave no contact/shadow cue.

Fix: Add an audit ground plane at the intended sole level and check rest plus sampled poses without changing the native root convention by guesswork.

Q03 · Geometry validity was confused with art progress

Closed meshes, contact tests, equal counts and many revisions were reported without a sufficiently strict visual rejection step.

Fix: Keep those checks, but reject obvious silhouette/material mismatches before calling a pass ready; close technical defects without calling them concept matching.

Q04 · The run-count estimate had no dependable basis

The earlier 85% projection was not supported by a stable measured improvement rate, and a fundamental head mistake survived several passes.

Fix: Withdraw the numerical completion forecast and use this discrepancy list with visible correction evidence and user art review.
