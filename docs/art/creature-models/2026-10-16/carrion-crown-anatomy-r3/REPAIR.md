# Carrion Crown r3 — anatomy rebuild

New construction replaces r2's solid barrel and flat flower mask with a closed thin-walled timber shell containing a real irregular front opening, eight buttress roots, a deep central crown cup, four grafted secondary cups, uneven rim teeth, rear crown timber, four grafted branch arms, six individually hung amber pods, diagonal abdominal ribs and hanging lichen. Broken broad lips are part of the cup wall; the rejected paddle-like added petals are absent. Owned bark/ivory/cavity/amber surfaces and pore normals are packed into four 512px maps. The saved source has 98 named supports, 25,480 closing-face samples and 26 rest/action poses checked. Trunk and attachment ownership were corrected after the initial cast-pose separations; no tolerance was relaxed.

## Remaining work

This starts Carrion Crown refinement; it is not adequate art acceptance. The cups still need more reference-specific overlapping torn sepal shapes and the body needs denser crooked timber, attached tissue, less regular rib/branch rhythms, clearer porous seed forms and more deliberate bark/lichen. The source remains a compact anatomy study, with native deformation and performance unverified.

Adequately reworked bosses remain **1/6**. No native loader, installed model, collision, every-frame deformation or live performance acceptance is claimed. The First Bloom and paused Blackwater Maw were not edited.
