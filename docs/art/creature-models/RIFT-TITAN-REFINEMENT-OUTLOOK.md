anatomical groupings agree, the important fractured stone/metal/crystal
features read correctly, and differences are mainly secondary chips and
surface nuances. A valid mesh or passing budget check is not concept matching.
No automated pixel score is used: the concept's perspective, pose and lighting
vary across views, and pixel overlap would reward incorrect interpretations.
The user's visual judgment remains the acceptance authority.
