# Magenheim shared asset design language

[Open the 3 October reference gallery](2026-10-03/gallery.html). The new sheets
are proposals for review. The [48 adopted creature targets](../creature-concepts/README.md)
remain the creature construction baseline. Approved staff and crystal weapon
identities remain intact; Underworld variant sheets explore their fittings and
materials, and do not authorize replacing base silhouettes, grips or dimensions.

## One reference identity per asset family

Reuse existing targets before generating another. Deep Fracture elemental
variants share thirteen chassis targets; the twelve elemental Surtlings use
six distinct elemental pair sheets. Their builds differ in mass and proportion:
Earth is massive, Fire athletic, Water fluid, Wind lean, Radiance classical and
Umbral elongated. Shared bone-role contracts do not mean one recolored body.
The initial shared-body Surtling board is rejected. Repeated legacy model aliases, color variants and the retired
generated armor do not acquire competing designs. The six biome material sheets
govern material identity; their appearances on a tool, bench or armor sheet are
use-context studies, not separate material designs. The gallery and selected
manifest govern current references, not a search for every PNG in the archive.

## A visible material lineage

Each biome links gathered resources, processed stock, equipment, its working
station and its surrounding ecology. Keep the same physical grain, strata,
fibres and palette across those uses. A Worldroot weapon fitting should look
made from the Worldroot plank on the bench and related to the harvested trunk.
Processing changes shape and surface finish, rather than simply increasing glow.

Fungal Forest uses pale ivory exposed Worldroot flesh, weathered dark exterior
bark, waxy fungal tissue and woven pale cord. Neutral inspection light must
show that intrinsic pale wood color; warm illumination can tint it in-world.
Blackwater uses wet layered stone, nacre, salt and water-shedding fibre.
Sulfur uses pitted slag, charred root and hammered dark iron. Frozen Caverns
uses frosted silver, laminated pale root and clear directional ice fractures.
Fracture uses stratified shale, aged ivory bone and sparingly exposed prisms.
Great Decay uses rot-stained wood, bone composite and translucent amber seals.
Material patterns must have comparable physical density on small pickups,
character equipment and large structures; avoid stretched or checkerboard grain.

## Form must explain use

Stations have working surfaces, input holders, connected tools and an intelligible
process. The Tidal Basin has an actual inset bowl and drain. The Mycelial Bench
has fibre and fungal preparation equipment. The Anchor Forge's crank mounts on
an axle, and samples sit on the tabletop. A drawer has a fitted front and seam,
not a cube with a handle. Storage, clamps, handles and illumination must have a
reason to be where they are.

Keep benches compact and reachable from ground level. Working edges and the
player's feet remain accessible. Use wear from hands, impact, cutting, water
or heat to break flat perfection; do not scatter unrelated decorative shapes.
Concept scale figures and drawn mechanisms are illustrative, not measured
collision or gameplay specifications. Preserve registered station siting rules.

Rootforged members remain visibly entwined roots. Substantial braided root
strands form beams, pillars, forked columns, braces, curved arch ribs and floor
supports; the kit must not become ordinary sawn lumber. Natural curves occupy
the span between deliberate connection endpoints. Bands bind the root bundles
and Understone stays stone. Use a few thick silhouette strands and painted bark
detail rather than dense thin tendrils. Horizontal beams are dense load-bearing
wood masses with fused root contours, not open rails, lattice or fencing. Retain
the current floor structure and underbeams; its walking face is flatter and calmer.

Understone foundations and pillar plinths follow the supplied hall reference:
massive dressed stone blocks, stepped feet/caps, recessed runic and interlace
relief, and fitted dark metal borders and corner straps. The monumental Worldroot
posts are a separate solid wood family mounting on those bases; they do not
replace the entwined-root pillars. Their exposed wood is pale ivory-white, like
birch wood flesh. The red seen in the source hall is warm illumination, not
reddish timber albedo. Inspect this material under neutral lighting.

Architecture uses repeatable, measured endpoints and a consistent attachment
grid. Arches meet the grid on both feet; neighboring floor timbers meet at seams.
Show load paths and joinery. Runtime collision and structural transfer need their
own validation; artwork cannot demonstrate they work. Deep Fracture interiors
stay enclosed and isolated from surface terrain. Reuse native cave entrance and
the native Aesir Gate identity; a forge is not a gate.

## Distill to Valheim production

Geometry earns its cost through silhouette, joints, openings, usable handles,
material boundaries or moving process parts. Fine grain, gills, pores, seams,
mineral striations and small scratches belong in owned texture maps. Avoid
identical primitive assemblies with incidental detail. Compare the actual native
donor and custom asset under consistent light, distance and framing, including
triangles, renderers, texture memory and animation cost. No numeric budget in
these concept drawings constitutes a measured performance result.

Use the project's owned material and texture sources when implementing these
targets. Rework their scale, mapping and authored detail where needed; do not
bypass them with donor textures or flat primitive colors. Reference artwork is
not a tileable texture or an excuse to substitute a new shader. Independent
meshes follow the supported native material template specified in AGENTS.md.

Armor retains native donor meshes, skinning, cloth and body overlays as required
by AGENTS.md. Use the set sheets to guide owned textures and material placement
on those shapes. Disregard any generated ornament or silhouette that would require
unsupported new skinning. Native donor topology wins over illustrative artwork.
The Diving Bell Hood likewise must conform to its retained wearable donor.

Mushroom canopies use broad silhouette surfaces and painted gills rather than
dense rings of geometry. Scattering uses reusable clusters; harvestable deposits
have distinct readable shapes. Foods look prepared and edible: the three catalog
recipes receive a sheet, but other-biome recipes are not implied or invented.

Small luminous cores remain bounded, without obscuring material form. Cloth,
stone and the character remain readable under ordinary lighting. Null Mantle
darkening belongs only to an equipped wearer; red eye accents sit at the actual
eye sockets. Surface/Underworld royalty behavior remains a separate code contract.

## Review and preservation

Resolve inconsistent generated views, unreadable labels and unsupported details
into an explicit construction drawing before authoring. The exact named-member
brief is authoritative over erroneous image text. The coverage ledger distinguishes
individual targets, shared family direction, retained approved bases and inherited
effects; it is not a claim of individual artwork for every catalog variant.

Full-size selected PNGs, exact prompts, receipts and hashes are kept together in
the dated directory. New revisions get sibling filenames and explicit selection
records; never silently replace the adopted creature set or approved weapon base.
These images are reference documentation, not installed textures or models.

The user's [Underworld atmosphere reference](2026-10-03/references/README.md)
governs lighting mood: dark green depth, localized warm orange vertical light
and readable silhouettes. Neutral parchment asset studies are not the intended
world illumination. Preserve underground enclosure and restrained highlights.

[weapon-ancestry.json](2026-10-03/weapon-ancestry.json) preserves the twelve
registered derivative-to-parent relationships and parent source hashes. Those
parent files, rather than an artist's guessed silhouette, govern construction.
