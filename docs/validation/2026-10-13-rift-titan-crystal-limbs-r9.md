# Rift Titan crystal and limb depth r9

The user requests additional crystals and more depth/detail on the arms and
legs after the r8 improvement. This permits the requested limb detail work;
it does not establish adequate boss acceptance. The tally remains 1/6.

Sixteen existing limb plates receive deeper staggered front/rear planes,
oblique face ridges and narrower splinter ends, without increasing their
triangle allocation. Proximal shoulder-blended plates remain intact. Twelve
new violet shards grow from upper-arm, forearm and leg plates; four formerly
floating shards now grow from wrists/lower legs. Bases were widened and tips
shortened after the first render looked too needle-like. The left-facing side
inspection then prompted turning their axes along the limb fractures, reducing
the excessively forward-projecting spikes. Complete basal
sections are embedded into their actual final stone hosts with identical bone
weights. All preceding collar, rib, band, neck, arm and digit roots are reseated
after the support surfaces are shaped.

The six thin internal joint bindings are simplified from 20 to 8 triangles
each while retaining their full axial reach and rig ownership. This frees 72
triangles for the twelve new six-triangle shards. It is a closed topology
condensation, not a boolean union or a claim that buried faces were culled.
The total remains 1,800, exactly the soft 1.5× cap against the 1,200-triangle
Stone Golem. One mesh/material, four packed 512px maps, original eight-bone
hierarchy and seven actions remain. No runtime promotion or installation.
