# Sulfurous Wastes Ashmite — primary-body UV atlas refinement (2026-10-10)

**Scope:** The established Ashmite thorax and abdomen only. The 2 × 5,760-triangle sculpted meshes, all 2 × 2,882 vertices, body anchors, closed topology, physical sternite divisions, heat fissures, silhouette and Thorax/Abdomen rig assignments remain unchanged. No new creature, resource, station, equipment, appendage or material is introduced.

**Root cause:** The body author previously used the entire `(u,v)` 0–1 space for the sidewall and projected both circular end caps into the same central texture area. At both ends, the end-cap triangles sampled the same heat-chitin PBR texels as the flank, causing avoidable overlapping UV surfaces and no dedicated material allocation for cap surfaces. The sidewall also sampled the outermost texture border, inviting bilinear/mipmap wrap bleed.

**Repair:** A new explicit `ashmite-body-three-island-uv-v1` contract allocates the sidewall to U 0.04–0.96 and V 0.045–0.615; the posterior and anterior caps receive separate radius-0.15 disks centered at (0.28,0.81) and (0.72,0.81). All islands have positive noncollapsed UV face area and 1024px PBR texture padding. Per-loop UVs preserve a seam at the lateral meridian without a full-width interpolation streak. The Blender author stamps the UV contract; the production verifier must reject missing or stale contracts and compare all UV loops against generated source.

**Offline evidence:** Nine Blender-independent primary-body regression tests pass (six original geometry/silhouette tests plus three atlas tests). Both body mesh triangle counts and exact geometry are preserved; this is texture-coordinate improvement, not new geometry or increased texture resolution. The attached orthographic atlas is generated from the actual procedural UV loops.

**Outstanding:** Existing committed `.blend`, GLB and runtime model are not regenerated by this source change. Actual installed Tick donor triangle/texture floors, Blender regeneration and verification, full approved-concept silhouette overlay, deformation across ten actions, and Valheim runtime/multiplayer inspection remain unverified. No production acceptance is claimed.
