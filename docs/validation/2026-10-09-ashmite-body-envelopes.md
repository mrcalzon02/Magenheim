# Ashmite thorax and abdomen primary-mass refinement — candidate, 2026-10-09

Scope is restricted to the established Sulfurous Wastes Ashmite's two existing primary body meshes. The former perturbed ico-spheres are replaced in the Blender author with distinct, closed, UV-authored 36-ring x 80-sector heat-chitin envelopes. The thorax retains its (0,0,0.085) origin and (0.095,0.105,0.052) nominal radii; the abdomen retains (0,-0.105,0.080) and (0.105,0.125,0.060). Both remain assigned to their original Thorax/Abdomen bones and Ashmite_HeatChitin PBR material. Five existing dorsal scutes, six legs, head, eyes and feeding tools are unchanged.

The refined primary masses have continuous asymmetric fore/aft fullness (not left/right asymmetry), low scute clearance, load-bearing lateral hip buttresses, physical lateral plate scallops aligned with the existing dorsal armor, two shallow curved heat fissures and underside sternite relief. No new organs, horns, or creature concepts are introduced.

Each envelope has 2,882 vertices and 5,760 triangles; the pair has 11,520 triangles versus 10,240 triangles for the previous level-4 ico-sphere pair. This is a *local replacement comparison*, not a verified installed Tick donor-quality measurement. Six Blender-independent tests check anatomical identity, triangle count, closed two-edge manifold, outward signed volume, scale envelope, bilateral symmetry, UV seams, fissure relief and ventral profile. The production Blender verifier additionally compares expected vertices, face winding and UV loops against the saved source, rejecting stale .blend output.

Offline orthographic QA includes the approved existing head/leg/scute anchors but is not a full-creature concept overlay or Blender/Valheim render. Source-art acceptance, Blender regeneration, actual donor skeleton triangle and texture dimensions, animation deformation, GLB regeneration, and live Valheim/multiplayer verification remain pending. Do not label the source production-accepted on geometry tests alone.
