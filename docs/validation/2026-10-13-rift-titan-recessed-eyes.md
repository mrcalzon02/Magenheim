# Rift Titan — recessed stone eye sockets (2026-10-13)

**Authority:** approved Rift Titan concept, `INSTRUCTIONS.md` boss tally, StoneGolem donor and existing unaccepted r3/r4 primary study.

The prior head candidate replaced anonymous floating strata with a brow, jaw, cheeks, and dark eye-well plates, but its two purple eyes projected **in front** of those solid well plates. This revision turns each eye well into a genuine inset cavity: a raised rectangular stone lip, an inner aperture, cavity walls and a closed rear wall. The eyes are shifted backward by 0.060 study units so their foremost point remains behind the stone lip while remaining visible inside the opening. The two wells remain attached to `Body`, retain `rift-bands` material and preserve the original crystal and action identities. This is a boss-head anatomy correction, not an added creature.

Each socket is 16 vertices / 28 triangles instead of 24 triangles. Two sockets add eight source triangles. The preceding unregenerated candidate projected 1,764 triangles; this source revision projects **1,772** versus the StoneGolem donor 1,200 and the boss-art soft cap 1,800. It does not override the separate ordinary elite runtime targets. The checked-in r4 study is 1,660 triangles; the new candidate has not been rebuilt. Four 512px atlas maps remain the latest measured textures, below the normal 2048px complex-creature source guidance.

Ten Blender-independent tests cover the head envelope, open core window, true cavity depth, mirrored placement, manifold topology, outward winding, positive volume, triangle accounting and invalid dimensions. They passed offline against equivalent geometry source. Front/side orthographic geometry inspection was produced, **not** a Blender render, concept-art acceptance or runtime proof.

**Still unverified:** Blender regeneration, actual source triangle count, authored UV and texture parity, GLB, LOD0/LOD1/LOD2, inherited animation deformation, donor visual comparison, native admission and Valheim performance. The Rift Titan remains **unaccepted**, and the boss tally remains **1/6**. No small creatures or non-creature assets were touched.
