# Rift Titan thin torso wrap and upper collar ring r6

The user rejected the thick front V plate in r5: the concept shows a very thin
sculpted band wrapped around a continuous rib cage both above and below it.
The next correction identifies the upper collar as a ring joining the larger
upper torso, rather than a flat stone bridge. User torso crops are preserved
with the new review gallery.

r6 replaces the front plate with a closed 12-station, 72-triangle stone ribbon
and a small V. Its authored height is 0.058 and radial depth 0.035 before the
uniform 7 m study scale. Twelve continuous metal ribs pass behind that band
from the upper collar ring to the lower keel. The upper collar is a closed
8-station, 48-triangle ring, with full left and right sections seated into the
existing shoulder/upper torso stones. Neck roots are reseated into its front
arc. Both stones and all torso supports use matching Body weights. Supports
are seated before dependent ribs; this fixes the contact separation caught
when collar seating was performed after rib seating in an intermediate build.
