# Ashmite paired-mandible PBR atlas separation — 2026-10-10

**Scope:** Existing Sulfurous Wastes Ashmite, its two established feeding mandibles only. No new anatomy, species, equipment, resource, station or gameplay behavior. The 410 vertices, 432 faces and 816 triangles per mandible, inward-returning hooks, three physical denticles, dorsal keel, root/tip anchors, Mandible_L/R bone bindings and `Ashmite_Mouthpart` PBR material are unchanged.

**Root cause:** The former cylindrical UV sidewalls filled the entire 0–1 square while the root and tip caps both occupied the same centered radius-0.48 disk. Consequently all three distinct physical surfaces sampled overlapping texels from the 1024px mouthpart albedo, normal and roughness maps. The sidewalls also touched the 0/1 texture border, inviting mipmap and bilinear wrap bleeding.

**Authoring repair:** `ashmite-mandible-three-island-uv-v1` reserves U .04–.96 and V .045–.635 for the sidewall, a radius-.13 disk at (.27,.82) for the root cap, and a separate radius-.13 disk at (.73,.82) for the tip cap. The vertical gap to the cap disks is .055 UV (~56px at 1024px); the gap between cap disks is .20 UV (~205px). Every polygon has nonzero UV area, each cap is disjoint from the sidewall and the other cap, and the lateral cylindrical seam retains a deliberate UV discontinuity. Both mirrored mandibles preserve their existing face winding and vertex positions. This does not increase the source texture resolution or the mesh triangle count.

**Verification:** Blender-independent four-test UV atlas suite covers contract, counts, anchors, island bounds and gutters, UV face area, finite coordinates and sidewall seam. The Blender author stamps a separate UV contract, and the production verifier now compares every authored vertex, polygon and UV loop to source geometry, rejects missing/stale UV contracts, and checks the mouthpart material. Orthographic UV and dorsal geometry QA are offline source inspections, not accepted Blender renders.

**Production blockers:** No approved Ashmite concept artwork was located in the inspected repository documents. The committed `.blend`, GLB and runtime payload have not been regenerated. The installed Valheim Tick donor polygon/texture minimums remain unmeasured; animation deformation, complete creature silhouette overlay and Valheim runtime/multiplayer inspection remain open. No art-complete or donor-floor acceptance is claimed.
