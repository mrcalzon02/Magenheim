# Rift Titan primary r3 — shoulder and limb proportions (2026-10-12)

**Authority:** INSTRUCTIONS.md boss tally, docs/art/creature-models/REFINEMENT-STANDARDS.md, adopted Rift Titan concept at docs/art/creature-concepts/2026-10-03/the-rift-titan.png, and the existing r3 handoff. This is the next explicitly documented r3 clay-review correction, not a new creature or a redesign of the Rift Colossus donor rig.

The existing primary study's shoulders used three overlapping plates with half-depths 0.29, 0.25 and 0.21 m; the revised authoring layout uses 0.18, 0.15 and 0.12 m while retaining the broad lateral reach. All six arm/forearm/leg bone segments now receive three narrower and shallower strata aligned to their **actual X/Z bone direction**, instead of short near-vertical block profiles. Their long-axis length is 53–60% of each segment, and the section width is capped by the segment length, so short bones cannot produce broad toy blocks. The dark binding threads, open chest cage, crown, pelvis, rig assignments, action identities, object names, materials and mesh counts are unchanged.
