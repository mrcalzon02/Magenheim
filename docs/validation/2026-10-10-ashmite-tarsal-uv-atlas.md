# Ashmite terminal-foot PBR UV atlas separation — 2026-10-10

**Scope:** The established Sulfurous Wastes Ashmite's six protective tarsal shells and six backward-facing mineral rasps. No additional creature, anatomy, station, equipment or gameplay behavior. Existing `ashmite-tarsal-rasp-six-pairs-v1` geometry contract, 20 radial sides, 11/17 axial rings, original left/right foot anchors, protected-joint and mouthpart materials, and `L/R_Tarsus1..3` bindings remain unchanged. Each tarsus still has 440 triangles and each scraper 680; the twelve parts total **6,720 triangles**.

**Defect:** The original tarsal UVs mapped each sidewall over the full 0–1 square, while root and tip caps both occupied a shared centered radius-0.48 disk. This caused all three anatomically different surfaces to sample overlapping texels from the 1024px protected-joint/mouthpart PBR maps, with sidewall coordinates touching the wrap/mipmap boundaries.

**Repair:** `ashmite-tarsal-three-island-uv-v1` reserves U .04–.96 and V .045–.635 for the sidewall; root and tip caps occupy separate radius-.13 disks at (.27,.82) and (.73,.82). The vertical sidewall-to-cap gutter is .055 UV (~56px at 1024px), and the cap-to-cap gutter is .20 UV (~205px). No vertex, polygon, skeleton, silhouette, material assignment or source texture resolution changed. The Blender author stamps the new UV contract on all twelve parts, and the production verifier now compares their complete vertex, face and UV-loop arrays against authoritative procedural geometry, rejecting stale `.blend` assets and mapping drift.

**Offline evidence:** Five new tests cover all twelve mesh instances: unchanged geometry fingerprints and rig anchors, per-island containment/gutters, nondegenerate bounded UV faces, cylindrical seam discontinuity, and separate root/tip texel regions. All passed in a Blender-free run. The QA atlas is generated from the actual procedural UV loops. This is authoring integration, **not** regenerated Blender/GLB or Valheim runtime acceptance.
