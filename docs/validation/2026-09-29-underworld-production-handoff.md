The equipment economy is also source-complete through all six biomes:

- Fungal uses the existing Mycelial Bench refinements.
- Blackwater, Sulfur, Frozen, Fracture and Great Decay each own three processed materials at their
  canonical station.
- all post-Fungal tools, armour and Underworld weapon recipes consume processed station output
  instead of jumping directly from raw pickups to end-game gear.
- all twelve Underworld weapon definitions are runtime-registered.
- all six biome tools are runtime-admitted with distinct mechanics.
- armour donor stats are overwritten by the explicit six-tier Magenheim balance table rather than
  inheriting Carapace/Feather values.

  Palewater additionally reduces Blackwater swimming stamina use, and Stoneanchor reduces incoming
  knockback in Fracture Zones.

Station siting is executable rather than descriptive: canonical-biome checks, Blackwater shoreline
sampling, live geothermal-vent adjacency, deterministic quiet/stable terrain, deployed Anchor Spike
stabilization and Decay Deepstone attunement all feed the Core placement authority and are rechecked
