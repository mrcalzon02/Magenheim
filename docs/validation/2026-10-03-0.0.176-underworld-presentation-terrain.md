# 0.0.176 integrated Underworld repair

The user confirmed 0.0.175 loads and runs the paired world. This candidate retains
its native world-generation and saved-chunk repairs and addresses the new visual,
terrain, pickup and vegetation reports.

- Additive local physics scenes do not isolate rendering. The shared camera drew
  Surface scenery at the same coordinates as Underworld scenery. Surface renderers
  are now suppressed while the local view is underground and restored on return.
  Production-linked tests cover the other world, roof exemptions and original hidden
  states. This avoids toggling every Surface renderer twice per frame.
- Apply the owned cavern roof at camera pre-cull after native camera updates. Extend
  its far clip to include the 18,000 m roof. Remove the cloned native cloud overlay
  that follows Surface shader globals. Hide the installed YggdrasilBranch and
  yggdrasil_branch renderer hierarchies reversibly. Native names were read from assets.
- The terrain domain now uses the installed Surface water-edge radius, 10,500 m.
  Regional relief and rare massif amplitudes are multiplied by 0.2. Hydrology remains
  functional. Existing player builds and saved placements retain their positions;
  a fresh disposable world is required to review the whole new landscape cleanly.
- All six bosses require at least 512 m from origin. Saved owned boss sites inside
  that buffer are regenerated with the native location iterator; obsolete center
  proxies do not compose arenas. Existing boss ZDOs can move to the replacement
  site without losing identity/health. Ordinary locations and generated zones remain.
- Native Minimap.GetPixelColor lacks our six reserved biome entries. The patch adds
  six owned colors and leaves Surface colors to the original method.
- Authored Glowcap Flesh is only 18 cm high. Ground Pickable bodies now have at least
  a 40 x 50 x 40 cm interaction box extending above ground and retain the donor
  interaction layer. Dropped items retain tight solid bodies and native pickup/ownership
  rules. Native call inspection confirmed AutoPickup runs under scoped Player.FixedUpdate
  and Unity's overlap query dispatches through the routed default PhysicsScene. The
  reported live pickup failure is not claimed reproduced; pickup acceptance is pending.
- Add six harvestable Glowcap/Spirestalk variants using their existing native tree,
  log, stump and yield behavior. Register eight owned ground-cover types inactive in
  the prefab container before enabling world clones. Add native MineRock_Stone
  Understone deposits with owned material/yield and require their catalog entry.
- All 24 Fungal Forest payloads are rebuilt below the measured 596-triangle Fir
  near-mesh budget, retaining owned textures and applying a native LODGroup culling
  threshold of 0.10. Main canopies are 574–578 triangles; ground cover is smaller.
  See `2026-10-03-fungal-tree-comparison.md` for installed native measurements and
  previous/current counts. Pine is an expensive native outlier. Intermediate/billboard
  LODs and equivalent live FPS are not claimed.
- Include the prepared serialized creature-rig clone initialization repair; the
  latest log has 170 animation Find exceptions. Weather teardown clears only its own
  forced environment without calling EnvMan.FixedUpdate after its lights are destroyed.
- Null Mantle gating uses the actual local helmet slot; see the companion equipment note.
